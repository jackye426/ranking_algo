# Expert Discovery: data, evidence and evaluation

This is a professional-discovery preview. Source-backed relevance, qualification, independence approval and confirmed participation are separate concepts. Scarlet is a reference persona; no partnership or knowledge of its internal processes is implied.

## What the data can and cannot establish

The source projection contains professional descriptions, clinical interests, procedures, alternate biographies, practice information, research fields and registration identifiers. It excludes patient records, appointment history, private booking data and personal contact fields. The raw-row count is not an expert count or an approved roster.

The full source is audited before indexing. Stable identities use corroborated registration/profile evidence; source rows are preserved and ambiguous combinations are held for review. Valid HCPC registration is retained under HCPC. A numerical HCPC suffix accidentally copied into a GMC field must not create a second claimed registration. Profile identifiers are not silently promoted to regulator-verified registrations.

Evidence remains attached to its original candidate, source field and available URL. Alternate biographies remain separately attributable. A page link identifying a person does not automatically substantiate every merged fact about them. Missing source dates remain missing; the date the database was merged and the date a page was observed are not dates of clinical activity.

| Requirement | Available evidence | Qualification boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Clinical use and modality | Clinical interests, described procedures, explicit reporting/interpretation activity | A training placement or a broad specialty alone does not establish current practice. |
| Population and care setting | Explicit descriptions of patients or practice context | A mentioned institution or prior training location is not proof of experience in the proposed workflow. |
| Research or study evaluation | Research descriptions and targeted identifiable publications | A topic in a research-interests field or a PubMed search link is not verified authorship or a study role. |
| Procedure activity | Recorded activity, range and period where present | Combined CT/MRI volume is not CT-only volume. Undated counts and derived midpoints are not comparable quality scores. |
| Professional relationships | Explicit, sourced affiliations or advisory roles | Relationship evidence is not a conflict determination; absence of evidence never means conflict-free. |
| Current practice, availability and formal approval | Usually not established by these public profiles | Ask and record the recruiting team's engagement-specific decisions. AI cannot approve candidates. |

## Retrieval and evidence handling

Keyword retrieval uses the Robertson BM25 formula, with `k1=1.5` and `b=0.75`. Semantic retrieval uses actual local MiniLM embeddings. Candidate evidence is merged and reranked against the active assessment brief. Metadata, qualification dates and clinical requirements remain distinguishable; professional network/booking membership does not boost an expert's relevance.

Essential, preferred and unresolved requirements remain visible. Source passages appear immediately; optional DeepSeek explanations enrich them without being the sole evidence store. No model is allowed to create an availability claim, approval status, manufacturer relationship or regulatory classification.

## Evaluation protocol

The original diagnostic sample consists of 100 identity groups and 200 complete evidence passages. It deliberately includes ambiguous identities, duplicate groups, HCPC records, sparse profiles, alternate biographies, research, publication references and volumes. Its immutable decision ledger records both accepted cases and defects. Once those defects guide corrections, this sample is a development regression set, not an untouched holdout.

A disjoint held-out sample was frozen and reviewed once against `expert-corpus-v1-20b4182587fef2073ade`. It excluded every source record and exact claim text used by the initial sample. Its immutable `heldout-review.cjs` records 100/100 source-consistent identity decisions and 200/200 source-text fidelity, but only **168/200 acceptable claim treatments (84%)**. The first holdout therefore failed. Faithfully copied text was sometimes interpreted too strongly: training received became teaching, a research-interest topic became performed research, or a journal/institution/membership name became clinical expertise. Personal hobby fragments and empty or corrupted excerpts also remained.

These findings are not hidden by the fidelity score. General corrections and post-hoc regression results must be labelled separately. A second disjoint sample is frozen from new source anchors before reviewing the corrected handling; all source rows and exact texts from both earlier samples are excluded. Its content is not inspected until the normalizer is frozen. Any pass belongs to that independent second sample, not a rewritten first result. Conservative withholding counts as safe handling but reduces retained useful evidence, which is reported separately. Neither held-out source audit constitutes fresh public-page or regulator verification.

The second independent review is also recorded without rewriting its result. Against `expert-corpus-v1-f2535777eae0757b5463`, its 100 identity decisions were source-consistent and all 200 claim anchors remained sourced, represented by 219 current passages. **Source-support precision was 194/200 (97%)**, below the 98% gate. Six interpretation defects remained, including insurer payment being treated as a professional relationship, a clinical pharmacologist role being mistaken for an industry relationship, procedure dimensions being tagged as activity, and a paper title about a training model being treated as received training. Two further display/classification issues made safe treatment 192/200 (96%). Nineteen conservative extraction misses, including real company relationships and research/teaching work, are reported separately as usefulness limitations; they are not labelled invented credentials. No anchors were discarded to raise precision.

Round two has no source-row or underlying candidate-identity overlap with either earlier sample. The first holdout was source-row/text disjoint but shared one underlying identity through a different provider record with the development sample; that limit is retained alongside its failed result.

The third independent sample excluded all previous candidate identities, source rows and exact texts. Its anchors were frozen before the next corrections and reviewed against `expert-corpus-v1-27bba10511f365b7159e`. All 200 anchors remained sourced (203 current passages); **claim source-support reached 196/200 (98%)**. This did **not** pass the release gate: **three of 100 identity groups contained critical source-association conflicts**. Targeted provider-page checks showed a surgeon and same-name nephrologist sharing a corrupted raw GMC association, Robert Moverley being combined with Robert Morley, and Enrico Clarke being linked to physiotherapist Eric Clarke. These were source-data contamination problems that the normalizer had failed to hold for review. Literal source copying cannot prove that a fact belongs to the right person.

The immutable `heldout-round-three-review.cjs` records these cases, four claim interpretation errors, seven display/usefulness quality issues and 20 additional conservative extraction misses. The claim errors concern training received versus delivered, fellowship training mistaken for research, and an institutional research-centre name being used as clinical-interest evidence. Primary provider pages were checked for the three suspicious identities; this is targeted investigation, not 100 externally verified identities or a fresh regulator audit. The first-pass result remains failed even after corrections. No selected claim was discarded or replaced to improve the score.

The fourth independent source audit on `expert-corpus-v1-b2eb21e32a2451a4d6ab` again failed. Eight of its 200 fixed anchors were held with their candidate identities, mostly because shortened or alternate given names lacked sufficient corroboration. The remaining 192 anchors produced 194 passages; all were text-traceable, but **187/192 emitted anchors (97.4%)** had supported interpretations. Withheld anchors are coverage losses, not successes in emitted-claim precision. An unresolved psychology/physiotherapy registration association remained, and generic healthcare-sector work was labelled an industry relationship. Audit supervision, generic webpage setting language, and therapeutic ultrasound used in cataract surgery were also interpreted too broadly. Twenty-five separate conservative usefulness misses and six display issues remain recorded. The independent first-pass ledger is retained in `heldout-round-four-review.cjs`; source-linked naming variants require corroboration rather than automatic repairs.

The fifth independent source audit on `expert-corpus-v1-df5d3dceb8c666e65512` observed no unresolved critical identity conflict in its 100 groups and retained all 200 claim anchors (205 current passages). Text fidelity was 200/200, but interpreted source support was **193/200 (96.5%, failed)**. A master's degree became research activity; awards and professional-body grants became industry relationships; teaching delivery became received training; an expressed surgical interest became practice; and a personal endurance-sport hobby became an athlete patient population. Eighteen additional quality issues and 29 conservative usefulness misses are recorded separately. The frozen fifth sample was disjoint by candidate identity, source row and exact text from every earlier sample. This is source-record review, not 100 fresh regulator checks. `heldout-round-five-review.cjs` preserves the first-pass decisions.

The conversational pack contains 14 development and 28 held-out fictional briefs across seven scenario families: cardiac CT, skin-lesion imaging, non-specialist settings, complementary panels, relationship review, clinical versus regulatory expertise, and insufficient information/evidence. Labels refer to actual source facts, not model-generated ideal answers. Holdout labels are frozen before retrieval tuning.

`evaluation/evaluate.cjs` runs actual BM25-only, semantic-only and hybrid search with the same downstream eligibility and reranking. It emits scenario fingerprints, corpus version, per-family results, known-candidate recall at 20, source-supported precision in the first five results, and trust checks. A failing metric is reported as a failure; neither labels nor thresholds are silently adjusted.

The first development run recovered 50% of the known references with hybrid search, compared with 58.3% for BM25; this failed recall and baseline-regression gates. After general relevance corrections, a later development run on corpus `27bba10511f365b7159e` reached 91.7% known-reference recall and 100% central-evidence precision, matching BM25 and semantic-only on those aggregate metrics. All 105 trust checks passed. These are development results, not held-out results, and that corpus subsequently failed the separate identity audit above. In 13 development cases, semantic retrieval added candidates to the pool, but none was unique to semantic retrieval within the hybrid top 20. Both methods actually ran; this small pack does not establish an incremental hybrid recall benefit. Later corpus checks are recorded below.

The first 28-case retrieval holdout on corpus `b2eb21e32a2451a4d6ab` is frozen as a failure: **89.13% known-reference recall**, **95.65% central-evidence precision**, and **213/213 trust checks**. Recall fell below the 90% gate. A supplied software context was dropped and one panel brief unnecessarily requested clarification; some source-reviewed cardiac references also ranked outside the first 20. The three retrieval modes tied on these aggregate metrics. General fixes and rechecks do not turn this original result into an independent pass.

A second, independently written 28-case retrieval pack (`holdout-round-two.cjs`) was frozen before those fixes, with four fictional briefs per family. It varies wording and refinements over the same four source-reviewed reference people, so it is not an independent clinical-domain sample. Its author knew the aggregate first result and general software-context failure category, but did not inspect first per-case ranked results. The evaluator tests supplied technology preservation, keeps a pack fingerprint, and writes separate `evaluation-holdout-round-two-first.json` and latest reports. Its expectations are withheld from the search implementation author until scoring.

The second 28-case retrieval holdout, on `expert-corpus-v1-df5d3dceb8c666e65512`, is also frozen as a failure: hybrid known-reference recall was **87.5%**, versus **85.42%** for each single method. Central-evidence precision was **100%** and **223/223 trust checks** passed for each method. All missed known references entered both retrieval pools; the shortfall was their final rank. The cardiac misses partly reflect additional coronary-disease requirements, for which several other clinicians have stronger explicit source evidence. The skin misses also expose a real weakness: supplied assessment/software/non-specialist context remains visible but does not materially distinguish candidates once the generic skin-lesion requirement drives ranking. See [the diagnosis](RETRIEVAL_DIAGNOSIS.md). The first score remains unchanged; later improvements on these cases are regression results, not an independent pass.

Semantic retrieval introduced unique candidates to 26 hybrid pools, but none appeared as a semantic-only candidate in a hybrid top 20. Hybrid's small recall advantage here came from changed relative scores of candidates both methods found, not recovery of a unique semantic candidate into the first 20. These narrow results do not prove broad hybrid superiority.

One final source sample and one final retrieval pack are frozen for the systemic correction phase. The source sample contains 100 new identity groups and 200 claim anchors, disjoint by candidate identity, source row and text from all six earlier source samples. Selection occurred before normalizer edits and retains the same high-risk research, relationship, training, procedure/activity and alternate-biography strata. The evaluator does not read the selected content until the normalizer is frozen. The final 28-case retrieval pack contains four cases per original family and preserves all four known reference people. The author had seen prior failures; the search agent had already implemented its new context mechanism while the new cases were being authored, but the evaluator did not inspect that implementation or any new ranked output. This timing limit is explicit in the pack. No cases were run before freezing. After systemic fixes and all prior-case regressions, this is the final independent case set: if it fails, record the unmet gate without generating another sample to obtain a pass.

The reference candidate set is not exhaustive. Recall measures recovery of known source-reviewed candidates, not all clinically suitable experts. Precision tests evidence for a central clinical requirement, not satisfaction of every essential requirement. Neither metric establishes clinical superiority or formal assessor qualification. Deterministic interpretation tests and live DeepSeek checks are separate results.

The final independent source review on `expert-corpus-v1-ee9449e83c05a7a62986` retained every fixed anchor: **100/100 source-consistent identity decisions, 200/200 source-text fidelity and 199/200 supported claim interpretations (99.5%)**. The numerical precision gate passed, but the **zero-critical gate failed**. One source sentence joined a diploma in operative endoscopy to a later current clinical role, promoting qualification evidence to documented endoscopy practice in the actual requirement matrix. `heldout-final-review.cjs` freezes this failure. Twenty display-quality issues and 55 conservative usefulness misses are separate from the unsupported claim. These checks do not constitute fresh regulator verification. Correcting or withholding the critical claim before display is necessary; a later targeted correction check must not be presented as a new independent pass.

The bounded correction has now been checked independently on the **same 200 anchors and 100 identities**, rebuilding only their 314 source rows. Identity handling was unchanged; 185 claim projections were unchanged and all 15 changed projections were reviewed. The joined qualification is now training, and endoscopy is **potential**, while the separately documented pre-term-birth clinic remains **documented**. The same safe distinction also holds for the legacy mixed passage. All 200 anchors remain present, and no critical source or identity finding remains in this fixed sample. `post-final-source-correction.cjs` records the code hashes and checks. This is a post-hoc correction regression; it does not replace the first independent 99.5% result or its critical failure. Deployment must rebuild with the corrected code.

The final independent 28-case retrieval run on that same corpus recovered **95.83%** of known references with hybrid search, versus **93.75% BM25-only** and **91.67% semantic-only**. Central-evidence precision was **100%** for all three. The recall, precision and baseline comparison gates passed, but **three of 228 trust/refinement checks failed**: removing a research requirement, retaining a skin-lesion requirement through a refinement, and retaining a named manufacturer. The overall first-pass result therefore remains failed in `evaluation-holdout-final-first.json`. Corrective parser tests and a full replay are post-fix regressions, not independent validation. Semantic retrieval contributed unique candidates to 26 candidate pools but none uniquely reached the hybrid top 20; the aggregate advantage came from reranking candidates shared by both pools, not demonstrated unique semantic recovery in the final shortlist.

The complete replay after those corrections, on **`expert-corpus-v1-fc67aacfcc3999fc230c`**, passed **228/228 selected trust/refinement checks** in each retrieval mode. Hybrid recall remained **95.83%**, BM25 **93.75%**, semantic **91.67%**, and central-evidence precision **100%**. `evaluation-holdout-final.json` records this successful regression; the original `evaluation-holdout-final-first.json` remains unchanged. The three failed behaviours are now corrected. The replay also covers clinical-context retention, explicit removals, manufacturer preservation, unsupported current-practice/regulatory claims, source ownership, clarification, relationship evidence and avoiding panel padding. Essential/preferred handling and broader constraint semantics are additionally exercised by the focused software tests. The reported full regression run passed **336 Expert Discovery tests and 243 patient-demo tests**; neither total is a count of independently reviewed clinical cases.

The hybrid advantage over BM25 is **2.08 percentage points** on this small pack, coming from one additional known reference recovered in `final-outside-04` (the non-specialist workflow family). No semantic-only candidate reached a hybrid top 20. This demonstrates a measured change in ordering when both retrieval signals are combined, not broad superiority, a new network of qualified assessors, or a proven improvement in human selection outcomes. The benchmark remains 28 fictional conversations over four reviewed reference people in two clinical areas.

The separate `post-systemic-regression-review.cjs` records review of **126 previously adverse claim anchors and 14 adverse identity groups** against the final first-pass corpus. The original critical interpretation and identity issues are corrected or quarantined; four previously adverse claim anchors are withheld with their identities. This is a regression check of known failures, not another holdout. Twenty-nine earlier display-quality findings remain, including truncated excerpts, generic marketing, personal-biography tails and placeholder addresses; two additional reviewed source fields still contain broken words even though their selected fragments were withheld. Conservative background classification also misses useful research, clinical activity and relationships. These limits must remain visible when describing data quality.

Held or missing reference identities remain visible in `referenceAvailability` and in the frozen recall denominator. Correctly withholding unsafe identities does not imply that search recovered them, and must not improve recall by silently shrinking its denominator. Earlier immutable reports retain their original scoring behaviour; new reports disclose the availability states.

Local embedding weights are required; evaluation does not send source records to a paid model. Coordinate full-index preparation so independent evaluators do not build competing copies in memory.

## Release interpretation

The engineering gates are source traceability, no critical identity/claim errors, at least 98% held-out source-supported claim precision, all critical refinement tests preserving essential/preferred distinctions, explicit constraints and removal semantics, at least 90% known-candidate recall at 20, at least 80% source-supported first-page precision, and no aggregate regression against the BM25 baseline. Report the named refinement tests that passed and their coverage limits; the pack's selected-label checks are not a measured accuracy rate for every possible free-form requirement. Source support includes the interpretation applied to the text, not merely copying its words. Useful extraction recall is a separate reported measure: cautious background classification is not a fabricated credential, but missing real relationships or work can still reduce discovery quality. Unknown/approval/relationship safety checks must all pass. Report corpus and sample sizes with every result.

**Data and deterministic retrieval conclusion:** the defined preview gates are met on the corrected implementation, with the qualifications above. The independent source precision measurement is **99.5%**, and its one critical defect was corrected and rechecked on the same fixed anchors before display. Earlier identity/claim defects are corrected or quarantined. The final full retrieval replay meets the numeric and selected trust/refinement gates. This conclusion combines independent first measurements with explicit post-fix regressions; it is **not a new independent zero-error audit**, a claim that the entire corpus has been externally verified, or a pass for optional AI-response reliability. Deployment health, hosted behaviour and model-backed wording remain separate release checks.

Prioritised work beyond the bounded preview is recorded in [the enrichment backlog](ENRICHMENT_BACKLOG.md). It focuses first on identity, useful task-specific evidence and scope, before larger corpus growth or additional model scans.

Engineering checks support a demonstration of discovery and qualification preparation. Assessment-team or clinical validation is a separate pilot activity. Current practice, willingness, fees, scope of approval and independence still require appropriate human qualification.
