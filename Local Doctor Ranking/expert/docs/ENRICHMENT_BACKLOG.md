# Expert Discovery: prioritised data work

The next investment should improve how much **attributable, task-specific evidence** is available for a shortlisting decision. More raw rows or generated summaries alone will not resolve the present gaps. Allocate the majority of the next development tranche to source review, extraction and evaluation; use targeted professional-source updates rather than a paid AI rescan of the entire corpus.

The current source-support result is 199/200 (99.5%) in the final independent audit. Its one critical interpretation error was corrected and checked on the same fixed anchors. This clears the bounded preview's data gate with a documented correction, not an independent zero-error certification. The final audit also found 55 conservative usefulness misses; the prior-case review retains 29 presentation issues. Those counts are sample findings, not estimates of corpus-wide prevalence.

## 1. Resolve identity and source attribution before adding evidence

**Why it matters:** text can be accurate on the wrong person's profile. The audits found wrong-person links and incorrect registration-body associations that literal text checks could not detect.

- Prioritise held identities that could meet actual briefs, including ambiguous aliases and conflicting linked profiles.
- Corroborate names, profession and typed registration against authoritative professional pages or the applicable regulator. A profile URL number is not a regulator identifier.
- Record the source URL, exact relevant excerpt, observation date, conflict and reviewer decision. Preserve the raw record; release a held identity only when evidence resolves the association.

**Finish when:** each released identity has a reviewable source chain, no unresolved cross-person association, and regression coverage for its general failure pattern. Unresolved cases remain held and visible as a coverage loss. Do not silently merge names or rewrite registrations to increase recall.

## 2. Recover clinical task, population and setting from real statements

**Why it matters:** a broad specialty or topic is often insufficient to determine whether someone can contribute to a specific device assessment. Conservative background classification currently hides some actual clinical work.

- Start with the existing cardiac CT and skin-image pathways. Seek explicit reporting/interpretation, clinical decision, patient population and workflow statements where current records lack them.
- For cardiac cases, distinguish documented cardiac CT work, coronary-disease practice and the particular assessment question. Do not force the familiar reference people above clinicians with stronger evidence.
- For skin cases, distinguish lesion treatment, image interpretation, dermoscopy, non-specialist decision support and study participation. Generic software context must not invent an imaging modality.
- Preserve the separate clauses for training received, clinical interests, actual activity, teaching and research. Keep the original passage available alongside structured attributes.

**Finish when:** a reviewer can identify the exact clause supporting each added requirement; mixed sentences do not transfer training, institutional names or personal hobbies into practice claims. Evaluate both useful recovery and unsupported assertion rates. Source support alone must not conceal conservative missed expertise.

## 3. Attribute research and relationships precisely

**Why it matters:** these are valuable for complementary panels and qualification questions, but weak proxies can create the strongest false impressions.

- Resolve targeted publications to title, DOI/PMID, author identity, affiliation, study design and explicit contribution. A search URL, degree or topic is not a research role; coauthorship alone is not diagnostic-study appraisal competence.
- Review explicit advisory, consulting, employment and financial relationships with the named organisation and dates when available. A prize, insurer payment or generic healthcare-sector description is not a manufacturer relationship.
- Retain historical relationships and study work with their dates and limits. Lack of a recorded relationship remains unknown, never conflict-free.

**Finish when:** each added research or relationship assertion has direct attributable evidence and a scope statement. Manufacturer-relevant links are surfaced for human review without a clinical ranking bonus, automatic exclusion or independence approval.

## 4. Add recency and scoped activity without inventing current status

**Why it matters:** the raw projection's merge dates do not establish when a professional performed an activity. Current practice, availability and willingness are usually not supplied by the corpus.

- Separate publication/update date, observation date and the period described by the source.
- Where genuinely recorded, store activity unit, range/count, period, modality and source. Keep combined CT/MRI totals combined and preserve missing periods.
- Use explicit team qualification records for current practice, availability, willingness and fees. Do not infer them from an undated biography or an active webpage.

**Finish when:** every surfaced activity statement retains its scope and missing fields; time-sensitive requirements stay unconfirmed until suitable evidence or an explicit reviewed team decision exists. Counts are not quality scores.

## 5. Repair source presentation while retaining provenance

**Why it matters:** fragments, generic marketing, unrelated personal biography and placeholder addresses make a sourced product harder to assess.

- Prioritise the recorded residual issues: joined/split sentences, truncated passages, corrupted words, concatenated memberships, personal-biography tails and literal `None` address values.
- Recover missing text from the original professional page where practical. Otherwise withhold the unusable segment without losing its audit reference.
- Keep useful professional continuations when removing booking or marketing fragments. Do not manufacture a polished quotation by completing missing words.

**Finish when:** the disclosed quality cases are resolved or explicitly withheld, retained clinical content remains source-exact, and coverage losses are counted. Readability fixes must not silently alter claim meaning.

## 6. Broaden the evidence benchmark before broad product claims

**Why it matters:** the current 28-case retrieval benchmark varies language and workflow, but its known references are only four people across two clinical areas. Its 2.08-point hybrid gain over BM25 is narrow.

For a future bounded evaluation tranche, target at least **50 additional source-reviewed identities across six clinical/device areas** and **100 task-specific claims**, including sparse records, allied professions, competing evidence, historical activity and genuinely unavailable expertise. These are proposed future deliverables, not completed coverage.

Freeze an evaluation set before the next tuning phase. Include hard negatives and alternatives that may deserve higher ranks than the current reference people. Measure known-reference recovery, central relevance, requirement-level support, useful evidence recovery and false assertion rates separately. Run actual BM25-only, semantic-only and hybrid ablations with the same downstream rules.

**Finish when:** publish the first score with its denominator and failure cases, keep critical corrections as separate regressions, and do not keep selecting new samples until one passes. Human assessment-team review is needed to evaluate shortlist usefulness; engineering metrics do not establish clinical or regulatory qualification.

## Work that belongs in the project, not inferred enrichment

Qualification, independence approval, invitation/response history, willingness, fees and confirmed participation remain explicit engagement records. The system can prepare questions and preserve evidence; it cannot populate those decisions from public biographies. Pilot measurements should assess time to a reviewable shortlist, evidence completeness, qualification effort and confirmed engagement outcomes without implying proven ROI.
