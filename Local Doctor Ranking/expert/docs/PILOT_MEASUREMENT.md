# Proposed pilot measurements

No productivity improvement or recruitment outcome has yet been established. These are proposed measurements for a prospective assessment-team pilot, not claims of proven ROI.

Use fictional or approved non-confidential assessment briefs. Compare the team's existing directory/profile workflow with Expert Discovery for the same requirements. Counterbalance the order to reduce the effect of learning the same candidate biographies. Record the date, source snapshot and brief version.

1. **Time to a defensible shortlist:** time to identify relevant options and record a source-backed rationale and outstanding qualification questions. A quickly produced unsupported list is not success.
2. **Evidence quality:** proportion of statements the reviewer can trace to the correct professional source; important omissions; unsupported current-practice, research-role or approval claims.
3. **Comparison usefulness:** whether reviewers can explain meaningful differences or complementary coverage without confusing a missing field with lack of experience.
4. **Qualification preparation:** useful candidate-specific questions, appropriate relationship-review prompts and the completeness of exported evidence packs.
5. **Recruitment progression:** user-recorded contacts, responses and participation only where a real authorised workflow exists. Do not infer any progress from saved candidates or drafted outreach.

Review results separately for practising clinicians, researchers and device-evaluation specialists, and for well-documented versus sparse records. Record when no defensible shortlist can be assembled. A useful honest gap is better than filling the requested number of experts with weak matches.

Before using confidential engagement material, separately agree authentication, ownership, retention and operational access controls. The public preview is for non-confidential professional discovery; no dossier-upload workflow is included.
