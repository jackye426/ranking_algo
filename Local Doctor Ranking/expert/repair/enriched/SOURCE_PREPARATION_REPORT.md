## Corrections that materially improve utility

**Cromwell qualification boundaries.** The provider JSON parser split some
qualifications into isolated words. Original saved HTML recovers 472 intact
qualification paragraphs, replacing 1,331 fragments. Identical mobile/desktop
nodes are accepted only when their text agrees. Seven missing or conflicting
paragraphs, 65 held identities and one name conflict remain unaccepted.

**Nuffield research context.** Detached PMIDs, introductions and meeting names
cannot serve as standalone expertise claims. The final packet groups literal
Research-list entries in their original order. It preserves profile-reported
bibliography and presentation context without asserting individual publication
verification, author roles or regulatory competence. Department-only awards
identified by review were withheld.

**Clinical field semantics.** Generic treatment fields contain diagnoses as well
as procedures; they now have a neutral treatment-listing type. Registers and
mixed professional-body lists no longer imply society membership. Academic
background remains neutral when it mixes training, research and employment.

**Professional-only scope.** Source-native truncation, malformed entities,
navigation contamination, punctuation-only records, personal history, phone
instructions and generic publisher relationship boilerplate are withheld.
Whole affected blocks are withheld rather than medically rewritten. The final
prepared-field scan also suppresses matching older primary/alternative wording.
These conservative exclusions can reduce background completeness; that is an
explicit limitation rather than a hidden text reconstruction.

**Dates and activity.** PHIN `last_updated_all` is a source-reported profile update
date from the upstream response. It is not the scraper time, a procedure
reporting period, proof of current practice or proof of engagement availability.
Existing PHIN activity ranges stay distinct from observed counts. No new PHIN
volume claims were accepted where the necessary hospital/period context was
unresolved. No midpoint was promoted to an observed count.

**Roles.** No suitable explicit profession field was recovered by substituting a
specialty or title heuristic. Source text about professional roles remains
searchable without forcing GMC conventions onto physiotherapists or dietitians.

## Independent reviews: failures preserved, corrections verified

The original semantic review examined 404 complete items from 204 records,
stratified across providers and new information types. It found 393/404 items
meaningful as standalone claims (97.28%); 319/404 classifications were appropriate
as originally proposed. That candidate failed. Its original decisions remain

A fresh confirmation used 404 complete items from 204 different records, with no
overlap with the first 204. It found 402/404 supported their label (99.50%) and

reviewer applied the actual runtime adapter to the frozen baseline and verified
that the nine unsafe wordings were absent from complete prepared rows. Original
96 remediation checks also passed again. Neither sample was relabelled after
correction. This is source-based agent review, not clinical credential approval
or human assessment-team research.

Every one of the 167,466 v8 new items was independently compared with its
original source: JSON field pointers or a second HTML parser, names, canonical
profile ownership, typed-identifier compatibility, source hashes, eligibility,

The early v1 report checked only 161,288 of 172,658 proposals because most Bupa
HTML was then sampled. The final all-HTML verification closes that coverage gap;
the early report is not cited as full-corpus proof.

The inherited R2 provider receipt covers all 47,875 provider items. A fresh full
check verified all 32,951 inherited Bupa items across 13,014 rows against their
original saved HTML, with zero failures. All 80,826 inherited context items are

check covered all 248,292 v8 context items. The later immutable packets only
withhold 11 items and add exact-text-bound display policies; they do not add
unverified wording. Literal source agreement and semantic review are separate
measurements; one cannot substitute for the other.

## Source-to-workflow utility findings

## Final preparation completeness and display safeguards

The first complete v9 projection audit found 705 accepted contexts with no
passage and 835 with token differences. These original failures are preserved.
Independent inspection identified both deliberate exclusions and real runtime
defects: short MD/TB/AF entries, clinical phrases beginning Contact/Callus,
book/publication headings, intact semicolon lists, short biographical fragments,
and misplaced duplicate-fragment removal. A literal numeric comparison such as
`<1%` followed by `>90%` was incorrectly treated as HTML; this was a critical
meaning change, corrected before release. The fix recognizes actual HTML tags
rather than discarding text between arbitrary comparison signs.

The final source-v12 proof exercises the actual release provenance and
`rowEvidence` functions for all **248,281 context items**. It records:

- **247,613** with all normalized source tokens preserved.
- **133** conservatively withheld and **535** partially retained.
- Every one of those **668** omissions has exact rejected-segment and source
  provenance accounting: **zero unexplained token losses** and **zero unreviewed
  short fragments**.

The exclusions include booking/navigation text, contacts, personal or mixed
personal paragraphs, placeholders, source headings and known malformed
fragments. A mixed paragraph may contain useful professional wording that
remains withheld with the rest. This is an explicit recovery backlog, not a
claim that all raw source text is indexed or all content is independently true.
